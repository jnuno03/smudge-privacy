Delete your BlurKin account

Memories shared. People protected.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

BlurKin accounts live on your device. There is no BlurKin application server and no cloud login for us to delete. To delete your account, open the app → Settings → Delete account. That wipes your email login, gallery, named faces, and drafts on this device. You do not need to email us to finish.

How to delete

  1. Open the BlurKin app on this device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the warning, then confirm.

Confirming deletion removes your email login, in-app gallery, named faces, and drafts from this device. We do not keep a backup, so this cannot be undone from our side.

What uninstalling does

Uninstalling BlurKin also removes remaining on-device app data, including local account data. Files you already saved to Photos or shared to another app are not deleted by BlurKin.

Subscriptions

An ad-free subscription is billed by Google Play or the App Store, not by a BlurKin account server. Cancel or change it where you bought it: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions on Android, or iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on iOS.

Questions

Email facephantomapp@gmail.com if you have questions. Emailing us is not required to complete account deletion.
